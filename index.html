ABOUT OUR SCHOOL

G.G.M Convent School provides quality education, discipline and a bright future for every student. The GGM Convent School (Gauhaniya, Prayagraj) was established in the year 2016. According to official CBSE records, its formal year of foundation is listed as 2018, but the school initially started its operations on July 7, 2016.
